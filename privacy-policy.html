Your Privacy Matters

VIDLORA is designed to provide browser-based direct video link functionality without maintaining its own cloud video storage or mandatory user accounts.

1. Introduction

Welcome to VIDLORA. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you visit or use our website.

By accessing VIDLORA, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue using the website.

2. Information We Collect

VIDLORA does not currently require users to create accounts or submit personal information to use its basic direct video link functionality.

However, technical information may be processed by GitHub Pages, your browser or other service providers involved in delivering the website.

Such information may include:

  • IP address and technical connection data.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Requested website pages and resources.
  • Security and diagnostic information.

3. Video URLs and Browser Processing

VIDLORA's initial downloader is designed to process supported direct video URLs in your browser.

VIDLORA does not intentionally upload video files to its own cloud storage or maintain a library of downloaded videos.

When you enter a video URL, your browser may contact the original video source to request media information or play the video.

The original source may receive technical information, including your IP address and browser request details.

We cannot control the privacy practices of external video sources.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

VIDLORA may use essential browser technologies to provide website functionality.

Third-party advertising, analytics or other services may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies when integrated.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

For more information, visit: Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies .
