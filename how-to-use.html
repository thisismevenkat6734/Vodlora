How to Use VIDLORA

A simple guide to previewing and downloading supported video links.

Before You Start

VIDLORA currently supports direct video file links that are accessible to your browser, particularly MP4 and WebM files.

Make sure you have permission to download the video. Do not use the service to download copyrighted or restricted content without authorization.

Step 1: Find a Direct Video URL

Obtain a direct link to a video file that you own or are authorized to download.

A direct video URL may end in an extension such as:

  • .mp4 — MPEG-4 video
  • .webm — WebM video

A normal webpage link is not necessarily a direct video file URL.

Step 2: Open VIDLORA

Visit the VIDLORA homepage using a modern browser on your mobile phone, tablet, or computer.

Step 3: Paste Your Video URL

Find the video URL input field on the homepage and paste your direct MP4 or WebM link.

Check that the URL is correct and belongs to a source you trust.

Step 4: Preview the Video

Use the preview option if available. Your browser may display the video if the source allows playback and provides the required access.

Step 5: Download the Video

If the video is supported and the source permits downloading, use the download option displayed by VIDLORA.

Depending on the source server and browser, the file may open in a new tab, play in the browser, or download to your device.

Step 6: Find Your Downloaded File

On most mobile devices, downloaded files can be found in the Downloads folder or your browser's download history. On a computer, check the browser's configured download location.

Troubleshooting

Video URL is not accepted

Check whether the URL is a direct MP4 or WebM file link. A webpage URL or unsupported file type may not work.

Video preview does not appear

The source server may block browser playback, require authentication, or restrict cross-origin access. Try another authorized direct video URL.

Download does not start

Some servers and browsers do not allow websites to force downloads of files hosted on other domains. The browser may open or play the video instead.

Video is unavailable

The original file may have been removed, the link may have expired, or the source server may be unavailable.

Important Limitations

  • VIDLORA is not a universal downloader for every website or social media platform.
  • It does not bypass DRM, paywalls, or access restrictions.
  • It does not guarantee that every direct URL will work.
  • Download availability depends on the source server and browser restrictions.
  • Offline PWA support does not mean remote videos are saved for offline viewing.
