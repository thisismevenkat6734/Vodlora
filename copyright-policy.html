Copyright Policy

Respecting creators, copyright owners, and intellectual property rights.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

VIDLORA respects intellectual property rights and expects users to do the same. This policy explains the responsibilities of users who access or download media using our website.

2. Content Ownership

Videos and other media accessed through URLs submitted by users belong to their respective copyright owners or licensors. VIDLORA does not claim ownership of third-party content.

3. User Responsibilities

Before downloading any content, users must ensure that they:

  • Own the content or have permission from the copyright owner.
  • Have a lawful right to download and use the content.
  • Respect applicable copyright laws and licensing conditions.
  • Do not redistribute or commercially exploit content without authorization.

4. Prohibited Copyright Violations

VIDLORA must not be used to facilitate unauthorized copying, distribution, or exploitation of copyrighted material. Users must not use the service to bypass DRM, paywalls, access restrictions, or other technical protection measures.

5. Reporting Copyright Concerns

If you believe that VIDLORA itself is hosting, displaying, or making available material that infringes your copyright, please contact us through the Contact page.

Please provide sufficient information for us to understand and review your concern, such as:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is affected.
  • The exact URL of the allegedly infringing material on VIDLORA, if applicable.
  • An explanation of why you believe the material infringes your rights.
  • A statement that the information you provide is accurate and that you are authorized to submit the report.

6. Review of Reports

We will review reports received through our available contact channel and take appropriate action where necessary. This may include investigating the reported issue, restricting access to relevant website content under our control, or responding to the reporter.

VIDLORA is a static website and does not host a user-uploaded video library. We may not control the third-party server that hosts a video file linked by a user.

7. Third-Party Websites

VIDLORA may help users access direct media URLs hosted by third-party servers. We do not control those servers or the content they distribute. Copyright complaints about material hosted elsewhere should also be directed to the relevant hosting provider or copyright owner contact channel.

8. No Endorsement or Ownership

Providing a tool to process a direct media URL does not mean VIDLORA owns, endorses, or grants permission to use the linked content. Users remain responsible for ensuring their actions are lawful.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Copyright Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact

For copyright-related concerns, please visit our Contact page. Include enough information for us to identify and review your concern.
