Frequently Asked Questions

Is VIDLORA free?

Yes. VIDLORA is designed as a free-to-use website.

Can I paste Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links?

VIDLORA can recognize supported URL patterns and attempt official embeds. Preview availability depends on platform permissions, privacy settings and browser restrictions.

Can I download from every platform?

No. Platform embeds do not provide download permission. Direct downloads are limited to authorized media files that the source permits your browser to access.

Can I select 144p, 360p, 720p or 1080p?

VIDLORA can display the resolution exposed by a direct playable source. Multiple quality choices are possible only when the source provides separate authorized files.

Does VIDLORA store videos?

VIDLORA does not provide cloud video storage. Videos are accessed from their original sources.

Why does a preview fail?

The source may block embedding, require login, restrict cross-origin access or no longer provide the video.
