Frequently Asked Questions

Is VIDLORA free?

Yes. The initial VIDLORA website is designed as a free-to-use service.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use the supported direct video download functionality without registering.

Which video formats are supported?

The initial version supports direct MP4 and WebM video links, subject to browser and source compatibility.

Can I download videos from every website?

No. Some websites restrict downloading or require authentication. VIDLORA does not bypass these restrictions.

Does VIDLORA store my videos?

VIDLORA does not provide cloud video storage. Supported media is handled through the user's browser and original source.

Why is my video not downloading?

The source server may block cross-origin requests, direct downloading or browser access. Try a different authorized direct video URL.
