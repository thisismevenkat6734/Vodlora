About VIDLORA

A simple online tool designed to make authorized direct video links easier to preview and download.

Who We Are

VIDLORA is a browser-based online tool focused on providing a simple experience for users who want to preview and download supported direct video links.

Our goal is to make supported video access straightforward through a clean, responsive and easy-to-use interface.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build an accessible and privacy-conscious online video utility that works across mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices.

We aim to keep the experience simple, transparent and respectful of content ownership and platform restrictions.

How VIDLORA Works

VIDLORA initially supports authorized direct MP4 and WebM video links.

  • Enter an authorized direct video URL.
  • Check whether the link is supported.
  • Preview the video when browser access is available.
  • Download when the original source permits it.

Video access and download availability depend on the source server, browser compatibility and applicable permissions.

Our Approach to Privacy

VIDLORA is designed without its own cloud video storage or mandatory user accounts.

The initial version handles supported direct video links through the user's browser rather than maintaining a video library on VIDLORA.

Browser, hosting and external service providers may still process technical information according to their respective policies. Please read our Privacy Policy for more information.
